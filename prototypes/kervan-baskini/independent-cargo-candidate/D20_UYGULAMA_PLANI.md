# D20 — measurable map design
User explicitly defers Android and real human tests. Focus on product quality and reproducible analysis. Baseline from actual D19 terrain cell function: route length/uninterrupted time, conservative ground-level 4-neighbor flood fill (2-block headroom), approach distance to wagon interaction radius, line of sight from attack base at eye level, shared fork/merge and tunnel clearance. Analyze baseline before any changes; compare after proposed improvements. Preserve two different risk profiles, spawn/extraction access, all swept geometry, no spawn traps. Add tunnel entrance masonry and deterministic terrain texture only when metrics/clearance support; avoid arbitrary cover changes without evidence. QA test-only analyzer, no product build or download, no live/KOD/RTS edits. Report limitations: geometry proxies are not PvP win rates or simulation of jump/grapple/combat. Record plans, evidence and manifest.
